About

The Engineer Behind the Code

Hi, I'm an AI Engineer who believes the best products are built by people who understand both the technology and the problem.

I specialize in shipping AI-powered applications that work in production — not demos that look great on social media but fall apart with real users. From RAG systems that handle thousands of queries to autonomous agents that replace hours of manual work, I build things that matter.

My approach is simple: start with the user problem, pick the right tool (not the trendy one), and ship something people actually want to use. Then iterate obsessively based on real feedback.
